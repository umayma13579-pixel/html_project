My College

I am currently studying at Borough of Manhattan Community College (BMCC), which is part of the City University of New York. The college is located in downtown Manhattan and has a very diverse and friendly environment. It’s a great place to study and meet new people.

I am pursuing an Associate of Science in Computer Science. My classes include C++ Programming, Computer Architecture, and Web Development. These courses are helping me understand how computers and software actually work in real life.

The teachers are very supportive and the labs are well equipped. I often stay after class to practice coding or to help classmates with their assignments. There are also tech clubs and events where students share projects and ideas.

Studying at BMCC has helped me improve my problem solving and teamwork skills. I plan to transfer to a four-year university after finishing my degree and continue studying computer science.
